## The story (exactly six words)

> **Obituary listed three husbands. Four attended.**

The entry is the six-word story itself, in `story.txt`. Nothing is built and nothing runs — the text is the whole entry.

## Why this line

Two sentences, one impossible headcount. The obituary names three husbands; a fourth person shows up. Every word earns its place: *obituary* sets the scene and the stakes, *three husbands* sketches a whole life, *Four attended* flips the count and forces the reader to solve the mystery — who was the fourth?

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is the entrypoint: it reads `story.txt`, checks the story is exactly six words, and prints it. Reading the file directly works too:

```sh
cat story.txt
```
